THE STARTING POINT

A portfolio with a point of view

As a Digital Enterprise Management student at UTM, I’m learning to look at digital work from more than one angle. The business goal matters. The technology matters. And the experience has to make sense to the person on the other side of the screen.

How I shaped the page

I built the homepage around one idea: make the connection between business and technology clear from the first screen. The oversized headline sets the tone, while the project cards give visitors a quick way to see what I’m exploring.

01

Find the thread

Start with the ideas that connect my program and interests.

02

Give it shape

Use a strong hierarchy, blue palette, and distinct project panels.

03

Make it work

Build with semantic HTML, CSS Grid, Flexbox, and mobile layouts.

What I’m taking forward

This first version is a starting point. The next step is adding more real project work and refining the site as I learn. I want the portfolio to grow with me, while keeping the focus on useful ideas and clear experiences.
